(This is info on the project, please read to get an understanding of the ending goal. The instructions for your assignment are after the overall info)

Your web application should provide a service for an individual, group, or a business. The web application should provide a service/information that does not yet exist, or implementation of an extension of an existing service.  The web application can improve the visibility of the stakeholder, enable better communication among the  individuals in the stakeholder group, improve the recruitment of people and funds to the stakeholder group,  or provide a service to individuals learning a subject.  

For this project, you need to find a real-life client for whom you are going to design and develop the  website. This will help you in two ways. First, you will experience developing a website for a different  person/group according to their need/requirement, similar to a real-life project. Second, the client can  provide you the content including texts and pictures, and you can focus on designing, developing and  improving (according to your classmates and client) the website.

Your submission will be a webpage like those you have done so far, integrated with those. Your ACTUAL project pages will be in your project folder, as a standalone site (with its own html, css, scripts, images, etc... and related folders). This assignment and it's parts, which are ABOUT the project, are part of your normal class/homework site and should look that way.

Your webpage should cover everything in the requirements below, in an organized, systematic manner. This is probably best done with a combination of lists (ordered and/or unordered) and headings (h3 and below, since h2 is for your page name, and h1 is for your site name). Come up with your own setup, and avoid excess spacing. Use your default css file- editing it as appropriate.

Once you have selected your client, think about the proposed content and interaction for the application.  You may want to look into website with similar purposes that you choose, and talk with your client.

A home page (index.html) 
Navigation Bar that enables single-clicks to each of the five pages (if you have more, submenus are OK, as appropriate)

The name of the page
Purpose of the page
Audience/Users of page (customer, admin, etc.)
What will be content of the page?
