Project Overview - BB's Affordable Bundles

Project Name

BB's Affordable Bundles - Premium Hair Products and Styling Guidance.

Project Overview

This web application will promote and support a small hair business named BB's Affordable Bundles. The website will help the client showcase hair products, explain product quality, and guide shoppers through the selection process.

The purpose of the application is to improve visibility, improve communication with potential customers, and provide clear product information in a clean and trustworthy layout.

Intended Users

  • Customers looking for quality wigs, bundles, and related hair products.
  • First-time hair buyers who need help choosing hair type, length, and care methods.
  • Returning customers comparing products and contacting the business for recommendations.

Overview of Site Content

  • Business introduction and product highlights.
  • Catalog-style pages for available hair products.
  • Product detail content with texture, length, and maintenance notes.
  • Hair care tips and usage guidance.
  • Contact and inquiry form for customer communication.
  • Photo gallery for visual examples.

Planned Pages and Navigation Structure

The project will include at least six pages. A persistent navigation bar in the header will provide single-click access to each page.

  • index.html (Home)
  • shop.html (All Products)
  • product-details.html (Featured Product Details)
  • hair-care.html (Care Guide)
  • about.html (Business Story and Policies)
  • contact.html (Inquiry Form)

Component Header and Footer Plan

  • Shared header component with site title and navigation links on every page.
  • Shared footer component with copyright, social/contact links, and HTML/CSS validation links.
  • Consistent layout and color system across all pages for readability and branding.

Dynamic Functionality Plan

  • Image Slideshow (index.html): Auto-advancing gallery with prev/next and dot navigation.
  • Product Filter (shop.html): Filter buttons show only cards matching the selected texture category.
  • Bundle Price Calculator (product-details.html): Live price estimate based on selected length and bundle count.
  • Form Validation (contact.html): JavaScript validation for required fields and proper email format before submit.

Timeline and Communication Plan

  • Weekly check-in with client by text/call for requirements and feedback updates.
  • Mid-development review with wireframe and early page samples.
  • Final content confirmation before project submission milestones.
