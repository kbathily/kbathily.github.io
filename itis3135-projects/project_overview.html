Project Overview - BB's Affordable Bundles

Project Name

BB's Affordable Bundles - Premium Hair Products and Styling Guidance.

Project Overview

This web application will promote and support a small hair business named BB's Affordable Bundles. The website will help the client showcase hair products, explain product quality, and guide shoppers through the selection process.

The purpose of the application is to improve visibility, improve communication with potential customers, and provide clear product information in a clean and trustworthy layout.

Intended Users

  • Customers looking for quality wigs, bundles, and related hair products.
  • First-time hair buyers who need help choosing hair type, length, and care methods.
  • Returning customers comparing products and contacting the business for recommendations.

Overview of Site Content

  • Business introduction and product highlights.
  • Catalog-style pages for available hair products.
  • Product detail content with texture, length, and maintenance notes.
  • Hair care tips and usage guidance.
  • Contact and inquiry form for customer communication.
  • Photo gallery for visual examples.

Planned Pages and Navigation Structure

The project will include at least six pages. A persistent navigation bar in the header will provide single-click access to each page.

  • index.html (Home)
  • shop.html (All Products)
  • product-details.html (Featured Product Details)
  • hair-care.html (Care Guide)
  • about.html (Business Story and Policies)
  • contact.html (Inquiry Form)

Component Header and Footer Plan

  • Shared header component with site title and navigation links on every page.
  • Shared footer component with copyright, social/contact links, and HTML/CSS validation links.
  • Consistent layout and color system across all pages for readability and branding.

Page Design Details

1) Home (index.html)

  • Purpose: Introduce BB's Bundles and present featured product categories.
  • Audience: New and returning customers.
  • Content: Hero banner, short business intro, featured products, testimonials preview.
  • User Data Entry: No.
  • Validation Needed: Not applicable.
  • Buttons/Links/Drop-downs: Buttons to shop categories and quick links to contact form.
  • Actions: Users navigate to product and contact pages.
  • Special Notes: First impression page emphasizing trust and quality.

2) Shop (shop.html)

  • Purpose: Display available products in a filterable catalog.
  • Audience: Customers searching by style, length, or texture.
  • Content: Product cards with image, title, key details, and learn-more links.
  • User Data Entry: Yes, via read-only filter controls (category, length, texture).
  • Validation Needed: Basic validation for filter selections.
  • Buttons/Links/Drop-downs: Drop-down filters and links to product detail page.
  • Actions: Filtering and browsing products without storing user data.
  • Special Notes: Uses client-provided images and descriptions.

3) Product Details (product-details.html)

  • Purpose: Explain a product in detail and answer common questions.
  • Audience: Buyers evaluating a specific item.
  • Content: Photo gallery, product specs, maintenance tips, pricing guidance.
  • User Data Entry: No direct data entry.
  • Validation Needed: Not applicable.
  • Buttons/Links/Drop-downs: Links to contact page and related products.
  • Actions: Image gallery interactions and navigation to inquiry form.
  • Special Notes: Must be mobile-friendly for image browsing.

4) Hair Care Tips (hair-care.html)

  • Purpose: Provide educational content on hair maintenance.
  • Audience: New buyers and customers needing care guidance.
  • Content: FAQs, step-by-step care routines, do/don't recommendations.
  • User Data Entry: No.
  • Validation Needed: Not applicable.
  • Buttons/Links/Drop-downs: Accordion sections and internal topic links.
  • Actions: Expand/collapse educational sections for readability.
  • Special Notes: Supports customer confidence and reduces confusion.

5) About (about.html)

  • Purpose: Share business mission, values, and service policies.
  • Audience: Visitors evaluating business credibility.
  • Content: Owner story, quality standards, shipping/returns overview.
  • User Data Entry: No.
  • Validation Needed: Not applicable.
  • Buttons/Links/Drop-downs: Links to shop and contact pages.
  • Actions: Navigate to product exploration and inquiry.
  • Special Notes: Tone should be professional and welcoming.

6) Contact (contact.html)

  • Purpose: Collect customer inquiries and consultation requests.
  • Audience: Potential and returning customers.
  • Content: Contact form fields and alternate communication options.
  • User Data Entry: Yes - name, email, preferred hair type, budget range, message.
  • Validation Needed: Required fields, email format, and message length checks.
  • Buttons/Links/Drop-downs: Submit button, optional category drop-down, policy link.
  • Actions: Client-side validation and form submission to a non-CRUD endpoint (such as a managed form service).
  • Special Notes: No custom database write operations per course constraint.

Dynamic Functionality Plan (JavaScript, jQuery, jQuery UI, AJAX)

  • Interactive Gallery (product-details.html): Thumbnail click updates main image and caption for product previews.
  • Form Validation (contact.html): JavaScript validation for required fields and proper email format before submit.
  • jQuery UI Accordion (hair-care.html): FAQs and care sections displayed with accordion behavior to reduce page clutter.
  • jQuery UI Dialog (shop.html): Quick-view modal for product highlights without leaving the catalog.
  • AJAX Content Loading (shop.html / index.html): Product or testimonial data loaded from JSON files for dynamic display without full page reload.
