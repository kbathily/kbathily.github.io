Hair Care Guide

Care Basics

Weekly Routine (How)

  1. Detangle gently before any wash step.
  2. Wash with lukewarm water and sulfate-free shampoo.
  3. Condition for 5-10 minutes and rinse thoroughly.
  4. Air dry or use low heat with protectant spray.

Do / Do Not

  • Do store bundles in a satin bag when not in use.
  • Do use a lightweight serum for shine control.
  • Do not sleep without wrapping your hair.
  • Do not overuse high heat tools daily.

When To Replace Bundles

With regular care, premium bundles can maintain quality for 8-12 months. Replace sooner if shedding, dryness, or tangling noticeably increases after normal maintenance.
