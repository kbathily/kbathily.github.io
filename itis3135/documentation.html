Brave Kudu Safari Documentation

This page provides technical documentation for planning and conducting safaris with Brave Kudu Adventures.

1. Safari Planning

  • Assess client preferences and physical capabilities.
  • Select appropriate routes based on season and wildlife migration.
  • Prepare vehicle checklists and emergency supplies.

2. Equipment Checklist

Essential items for each safari vehicle:

  • Binoculars and spotting scopes
  • First aid kit and communication devices
  • Water purification system and emergency rations

3. Wildlife Viewing Protocol

  1. Maintain safe distance from animals (minimum 100 meters for large mammals).
  2. Use low voices and avoid sudden movements.
  3. Document sightings with photos, not disturbing behavior.

4. Safety Guidelines

  • Stay with the group and follow guide instructions.
  • Wear appropriate clothing and sun protection.
  • Report any health issues or equipment problems immediately.
